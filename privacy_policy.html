Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ZDT Tailor ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Customer Data: Customer names, mobile numbers, and addresses that you enter into the App for measurement and billing purposes.
  • Measurement Data: Body measurements (e.g., chest, waist, length) and order details you create within the App.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for analytics and crash reporting.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Providing the App's core functionality — creating measurements, bills, and managing tailoring orders.
  • Storing your data locally on your device via SQLite database.
  • Displaying advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Improving the App through anonymous usage analytics.

Data Storage & Security

All customer and measurement data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your personal data to external servers. The data remains under your control at all times. Deleting the App will remove all locally stored data.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for serving advertisements. AdMob may collect device information and advertising IDs to provide relevant ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Advertising ID

This App uses the Advertising ID for serving personalized advertisements. You can reset or limit ad tracking at any time through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
